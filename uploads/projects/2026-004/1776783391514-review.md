## 1. Summary

The Griffin Residence is a proposed new two-story single-family dwelling with basement storage on Lot 518 at 795 Columbine Circle. The design is generally well-documented and demonstrates evident attention to WUI compliance, snow management, and site planning. However, several significant compliance issues require resolution before approval can be granted. Most critically, the proposed land coverage of 2,979 SF (within envelope) appears to exclude items that may bring the total at or very near the 3,500 SF limit, and the calculation methodology requires independent verification. The roof design presents low-slope sections (3:12) that warrant scrutiny relative to the open-area pitch requirement, and the proposed stucco material appears to exceed the 60% maximum on at least one elevation. The hot tub shown on the upper deck lacks a required screening plan, and physical material samples have not yet been submitted (though this is expected at Final). Several items also require clarification, including the precise dwelling size calculation relative to the basement, the garage interior dimensions, deck post sizing, and the adequacy of the construction zone fencing depiction. A revised and resubmitted set addressing these issues is recommended prior to approval.

## 2. Compliance Issues

### Land Coverage
- **Potential exceedance of 3,500 SF land coverage limit** *(Design Guidelines — Restrictions and Requirements: Land Coverage)*: The site plan (Sheet C1) reports a total land coverage of 2,897 SF within the building envelope, broken down as: Residence/Garage 1,823 SF, Car Decks & Stairs 494 SF, Front Deck & Stairs 166 SF, Overhangs 10 SF, and Wood Framed Driveway 124 SF — totaling 2,617 SF within-envelope. However, a separate line item "Banked Space" of 824 SF is listed, which, if counted, brings the total to 3,441 SF — close to but still below the 3,500 SF limit. The plan also shows Wood Porches of 100 SF and 98 SF (lower floor plan, Sheet A1), plus a Basement-level Deck of 50 SF. It is unclear whether these porches and the basement deck are included in the coverage total. If any of these are terraced/graded areas usable for recreation, they must be included per the guidelines. The basis of the coverage calculation must be explicitly clarified and shown to comply.

- **"Banked Space" is an undefined category** *(Design Guidelines — Restrictions and Requirements: Land Coverage)*: The coverage table on Sheet C1 lists 824 SF as "Banked Space" outside the building envelope, which is acknowledged as not counted toward envelope coverage. However, any terraced, graded, paved, or otherwise improved areas outside the building envelope are not permitted without prior EMACC approval and TC-TAC envelope revision. The nature, location, and use of this "Banked Space" must be clarified, as it may require a building envelope modification application.

### Dwelling Size
- **Dwelling size calculation requires clarification — possible exceedance of 5,000 SF maximum** *(Design Guidelines — Restrictions and Requirements: Dwelling Size)*: The plans report Total Living Area of 2,880 SF (Upper Floor 1,840 SF + Lower Floor 1,040 SF) plus Garage of 887 SF, totaling 3,767 SF for the two occupied floors. The basement "Storage — Unconditioned" space (Sheet A5) is labeled at approximately 898 SF. Per the guidelines, "unconditioned" spaces are excluded from dwelling size only if they qualify as "underfloor" (at or below grade, unfinished, unconditioned, with clear headroom less than 7 feet OR ground slope greater than 5%). The basement plan shows a finished landing, stairs, and a 50 SF deck — these suggest habitable access inconsistent with a true underfloor exclusion. The headroom in the basement storage must be documented. If headroom equals or exceeds 7 feet, the space must be included in the dwelling size calculation, which would bring the total to approximately 4,665 SF — still within the 5,000 SF maximum, but this must be confirmed and clearly shown on the plans.

### Roof Pitch
- **Roof pitch of 3:12 on portions of the structure may not comply with the open-area roof pitch requirement** *(Design Guidelines — Restrictions and Requirements: Roofs)*: The guidelines state that structures built in open areas should have the majority of their roof surfaces pitched at greater than 4:12. The roof plan (Sheet A5) and elevations (Sheets A2, A3) show significant roof areas pitched at 3:12, particularly over the upper floor. Lot 518 appears to be in a relatively open area of the subdivision. The applicant must demonstrate either that (a) the structure is not in an "open area" as intended by the guidelines, or (b) the majority of roof surfaces exceed 4:12. A roof area breakdown (square footage of each pitch) should be provided.

### Stucco / Material Percentages
- **Stucco appears to exceed the 60% maximum on at least one elevation** *(Design Guidelines — Restrictions and Requirements: Materials)*: Material "E" — grey 3-coat stucco — is shown extensively on the lower portions of all four elevations (Sheets A2, A3, A4). On the right and rear elevations in particular, the stucco appears to constitute well more than 60% of the exposed wall surface. The guidelines state stucco "may not exceed 60% of any totally exposed wall or facade." The applicant must provide a quantified breakdown of materials by percentage for each exposed wall/facade.

### Hot Tub Screening
- **Hot tub lacks required screening documentation** *(Design Guidelines — Restrictions and Requirements: Screening)*: A hot tub is clearly shown on the upper deck (Sheet A1, upper floor plan). The guidelines require hot tubs to be screened from neighbors' view and from public areas such as streets and hiking trails. No screening plan, screen wall, or privacy description is provided. The site plan does not demonstrate that the hot tub will be adequately screened given the uphill and exposed position of the upper deck.

### Deck Railing / Post Size
- **Deck post size requires confirmation** *(Design Guidelines — Restrictions and Requirements: Decks, Patios and Access Routes)*: The guidelines require deck supports to be not less than 6"×6". Sheet A1 (lower floor plan) references "8x8 POSTS -TYP." and "12x12 POSTS -TYP." in the garage/workshop area, which comply. However, Sheet A1 (upper floor plan) references "8x8 POSTS -TYP." for deck supports on the upper level — these also comply. The plans should confirm that ALL deck support posts throughout the structure meet the 6"×6" minimum, including the wood porches and rear deck.

- **Deck cross-bracing prohibition** *(Design Guidelines — Restrictions and Requirements: Decks, Patios and Access Routes)*: The plans and isometric views (Sheet A4) do not clearly show underpinning treatment for decks more than three feet above grade. The guidelines prohibit cross bracing and require either a continuous screen wall or approved alternative. The note on Sheet A2 states "NO CROSS BRACING AT DECKS," which is acknowledged, but the plans must explicitly show the required continuous screen wall or other compliant underpinning treatment for all decks exceeding 3 feet above grade.

### Exterior Lighting

The submitted site plan (Sheet C1) indicates "2' CONTOUR INTERVALS" uniformly. This does not meet the requirement for 1-foot contours adjacent to the structure.

### Internally Illuminated House Number
- **Internally illuminated house number notation** *(Design Guidelines — Restrictions and Requirements: Outside Lights)*: Sheet A2 (front elevation) includes a note "INTERNALLY ILLUMINATED HOUSE NUMBER RECOMMENDED." ## 3. Recommendations

The following changes are required before approval can be granted:

- **Land Coverage Calculation:** Revise the coverage table to explicitly account for all terraced/graded areas, wood porches (100 SF and 98 SF), the basement-level deck (50 SF), and any portion of "Banked Space" that constitutes improved area. Confirm total coverage does not exceed 3,500 SF. If it does, a Land Coverage Variance application per Exhibit C must be submitted simultaneously. Clarify the nature and permissibility of the 824 SF "Banked Space" outside the building envelope; if it constitutes any improvement, submit a building envelope modification application per Exhibit A.

- **Dwelling Size Calculation:** Document the clear headroom in the basement storage level. Provide a revised dwelling size calculation that either justifies the basement exclusion (with headroom dimensions demonstrating it qualifies as "underfloor" per the guidelines) or includes it and confirms the total remains under 5,000 SF.

- **Roof Pitch Compliance:** Provide a roof area summary by pitch (3:12 vs. 4:12) to demonstrate that the majority of roof surfaces exceed 4:12, or provide a written justification that Lot 518 is not in an "open area" as defined by the guidelines. If the majority of the roof area is at 3:12, the roof design must be revised to achieve predominantly 4:12 or greater pitches.

- **Stucco Percentage:** Provide a quantified percentage breakdown of stucco versus other materials for each exposed wall facade. Revise the design if stucco exceeds 60% on any exposed wall or facade.

- **Hot Tub Screening:** Add a hot tub screening plan to the site plan showing proposed screening elements (walls, landscaping, or structural elements) that will block views from neighboring properties, streets, and public areas. Show line-of-sight analysis if needed.

- **Deck Underpinning:** Revise elevations and details to explicitly show the required continuous screen wall or other compliant treatment concealing structural supports for all decks more than 3 feet above grade. Confirm no cross-bracing is used.

- **Contour Intervals:** Revise the site plan to show 1-foot contour intervals in the area adjacent to the structure, as required by the guidelines.

- **Construction Zone Fencing:** Add a clearly delineated construction zone boundary line (not to exceed 12 feet from structure, driveway, and cut/fill slopes) to the site plan, per the re-vegetation guidelines.

- **Exterior Lighting Locations:** Show all exterior light fixture locations on site plan and/or elevations. Catalog sheets for all proposed fixture types should be included in the Final submittal.

- **Garage Net Dimensions:** Add a note or dimension confirming net usable garage dimensions (exclusive of workshop) meet the 10 ft × 20 ft minimum per car.
