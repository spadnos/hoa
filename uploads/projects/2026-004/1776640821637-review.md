## 1. Summary

The Griffin Residence proposal for Lot 518 is a two-story new single-family dwelling with basement storage, a two-car garage, and multiple decks, totaling approximately 2,880 SF of living area and 887 SF of garage area. The design shows considerable attention to site planning, erosion control, and defensible space. However, the submittal contains several significant compliance issues that must be resolved before approval can be granted. Most critically, the proposed land coverage of 2,879 SF inside the building envelope plus 824 SF banked outside totals 3,703 SF — exceeding the 3,500 SF maximum — and a formal variance application will be required if the applicant wishes to proceed at this coverage. Additionally, the plans specify electric heat pump systems as the primary heating source, which is expressly prohibited by the guidelines. A hot tub is shown on the upper deck without confirmed screening provisions. Several required submittal items are absent from this preliminary package. These issues are addressable, and the design concept has merit, but revisions and/or a variance application are required before this project can proceed.

## 2. Compliance Issues

### Land Coverage
- **Land Coverage Exceeds 3,500 SF Maximum (Restrictions & Requirements — Land Coverage; Exhibit B):** The proposed coverage table shows a total land coverage of 3,703 SF (2,879 SF inside building envelope + 824 SF "banked"). The maximum permitted land coverage within the building envelope is 3,500 SF. The 824 SF of "banked" coverage outside the building envelope is not a recognized calculation category under the guidelines — *all* coverage within the building envelope counts toward the limit, and coverage outside the envelope is not permitted (see Building Envelopes section). A formal variance application per Exhibit B is required to exceed 3,500 SF. Note that variances are generally considered only up to a maximum of 3,850 SF (10% above limit), and 3,703 SF would fall within that range, but approval is not guaranteed.

- **Coverage Outside the Building Envelope (Restrictions & Requirements — Location & Building Envelopes):** The site plan and coverage table reference 824 SF of land coverage described as "banked" that appears to fall outside the building envelope. The guidelines require that 100% of all improvements, including patios and decks, be within the building envelope (with the exception of driveways and rip-rap). Any area outside the envelope that constitutes a patio, deck, or covered walk is non-compliant as submitted. The site plan must clearly delineate the building envelope boundary relative to all proposed improvements.

### Appliances / Mechanical Systems
- **Electric Heat Prohibited (Restrictions & Requirements — Appliances):** The floor plans and notes specify ducted heat pump units with AC and zones (lower floor) and a heat pump for the hydronic system as the primary heating sources. The guidelines explicitly state: *"Electric heat is NOT permitted."* All residences are required to have gas (propane) heat. While hydronic radiant systems are *encouraged* as an alternative method when used in conjunction with compliant gas heating, an all-electric heat pump system serving as the sole or primary heat source does not comply. The heating system must be redesigned to use gas (propane) as the primary heat source. Note: propane detectors are also required per the guidelines and must be shown on the plans.

### Hot Tub Screening
- **Hot Tub Screening Not Confirmed (Restrictions & Requirements — Screening):** A hot tub is shown on the upper floor deck (464 SF deck, upper floor plan). The guidelines require that hot tubs be screened from neighbors' view and from the view of public areas such as streets and hiking trails. No screening detail, fence, or enclosure is indicated on the plans. As no fencing is permitted on any lot or lot lines (Restrictions & Requirements — No Fencing), the screening method must be integrated into the architecture or landscaping and must be specifically shown and described.

### Appliances — Gas Requirements
- **Gas Appliances Not Confirmed (Restrictions & Requirements — Appliances):** The plans show an electric range with hood on the upper floor kitchen and do not specify gas cook tops, gas ovens, gas dryer, or gas water heater. The guidelines require gas (propane) dryers, gas heat, gas ovens, gas cook tops and ranges, and gas water heaters. The plans must be revised to show all required gas appliances, and gas piping plans must confirm service to each required appliance location.

### Deck Post Dimensions
- **Deck Post Size — Lower Floor Deck (Restrictions & Requirements — Decks, Patios and Access Routes):** The lower floor plan notes indicate "8x8 posts - TYP." for deck supports. The guidelines require deck supports to be of a dimension not less than 6" x 6", so 8x8 posts are compliant. However, the upper deck (464 SF) notes reference "6x6 posts" in one location — this should be verified across all decks to ensure all posts meet the 6"x6" minimum. Additionally, the guidelines prohibit cross bracing; a note on the elevations states "NO CROSS BRACING AT DECKS," which is consistent with the guidelines and is acknowledged positively.

### Roof Pitch — Potential Concern
- **Roof Pitch in Open Area (Restrictions & Requirements — Roofs):** The predominant roof pitches shown are 3:12 and 4:12. The guidelines state that structures built in open areas should have the majority of their roof surfaces pitched at greater than 4 in 12. Lot 518's position in the subdivision should be evaluated: if the lot is considered to be in an "open area," the majority of roof surfaces must exceed 4:12. The 3:12 roof sections visible on the roof plan may not satisfy this requirement. Flatter roofs in other locations "will be reviewed on their individual merit."

### Snow Shedding
- **Snow Shedding onto Decks (Restrictions & Requirements — Roofs; Decks, Patios and Access Routes):** The front elevation and isometric views show roof planes that appear to shed toward the large upper and lower decks. The guidelines explicitly state: *"Roof snow shedding onto decks and patios is prohibited."* The applicant must provide a snow shedding analysis diagram or narrative demonstrating that roof snow shed is directed away from all decks, patios, entrances, garage doors, railings, driveways, and uphill slopes.

### Stucco Limitation
- **Stucco Percentage (Restrictions & Requirements — Materials):** Stucco (Material E on the elevation legend) is listed as a primary exterior material and appears prominently on the lower level/foundation walls of all elevations. The guidelines limit stucco or plaster use to not more than 60% of any totally exposed wall or facade. The applicant must provide a wall-by-wall percentage calculation to confirm stucco does not exceed 60% on any exposed facade.

### Exterior Lighting
- **Exterior Light Bulb Wattage / Concealed Source Not Confirmed (Restrictions & Requirements — Outside Lights):** The plans reference "exterior lighting to be dark sky approved" and note a motion detector light over the garage, which is consistent with the guidelines. However, the guidelines require all exterior light bulbs (other than snow removal lighting) to be no greater than 40 watts, and all exterior lighting must have a concealed source. The location and types of exterior light fixtures are required on final plans; at preliminary stage this should be acknowledged and confirmed for final submittal. Additionally, the plans show what appears to be an "internally illuminated house number" noted on the front elevation — illuminated house numbers must comply with the concealed source requirement.

### Utility / Tank Prohibition
- **Propane Tank (Restrictions & Requirements — Utility Installations):** The plans require propane gas for all appliances per guidelines, but no propane storage location is shown. The guidelines state: *"Tanks are not permitted."* This appears to conflict with the requirement for propane appliances. Clarification is needed — Mountain Utilities (MU) supplies propane at Kirkwood; the applicant should confirm service connection method with MU and show utility connections on the site plan.

## 3. Recommendations

The following changes are required before approval can be granted:

- **Revise land coverage to 3,500 SF or less, OR submit a formal variance application per Exhibit B** with written justification, trade-off mitigations, and a detailed coverage breakdown. Clearly delineate the building envelope on the site plan and confirm all improvements (except driveway and rip-rap) fall within it.

- **Redesign the heating system to use gas (propane) as the required primary heat source** per the Appliances section. Hydronic systems may be retained as supplemental/distribution systems if served by a gas boiler. Remove or redesign the all-electric heat pump heating configuration. Show propane detectors with local alarm on the plans (propane detectors connected to outside service are strongly recommended).

- **Replace the electric range with a gas (propane) cook top and oven.** Confirm all other required gas appliances (dryer, water heater) are shown on floor plans and that gas piping plans reflect these locations.

- **Provide hot tub screening details** integrated into the architecture or landscaping (no fencing permitted) demonstrating screening from neighbors and public areas on all relevant elevations and the site plan.

- **Provide a snow shedding analysis** (plan or diagram) demonstrating that no roof snow is shed onto decks, patios, entrances, garage doors, driveways, railings, or uphill slopes. Revise roof design if any current roof planes shed onto prohibited areas.

- **Provide a stucco percentage calculation** for each exposed facade to confirm compliance with the 60% maximum (Materials section).

- **Confirm all deck post sizes are a minimum 6"x6"** on all decks; provide a schedule or note on the plans.

- **Confirm the roof pitch complies** with the open-area requirement (majority of surfaces > 4:12). If the lot is in an open area, revise 3:12 roof sections or provide justification for EMACC consideration on individual merit.

- **Coordinate propane service** with Mountain Utilities and show utility connection points on the site plan. Confirm no on-site storage tanks are proposed.

- **For Final Submittal:** Provide location and fixture types for all exterior lighting; confirm concealed-source compliance and 40-watt maximum for all non-snow-removal fixtures.
